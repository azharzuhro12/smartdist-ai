# Retail Store Operations & Distribution Network Guidelines

**Document ID:** SOP-OPS-004  
**Category:** Retail Store Operations & Distribution  
**Section:** Branch Hierarchy, Receiving Schedules & Emergency Transfers  
**Effective Date:** 2024-01-01  
**Last Updated:** 2024-11-01  
**Target Audience:** Store Managers, Regional Logistics Coordinators, Warehouse Supervisors  

---

## 1. Network Structure
The SmartDist retail network comprises 54 retail store branches across Ecuador, categorized into three distinct store formats:
- **Format A (Hypermarkets / Large format):** High daily throughput, warehouse storage on-site, daily replenishment delivery slots.
- **Format B (Supermarkets / Medium format):** Standard retail stores, deliveries 3 to 4 times per week.
- **Format C (Express / Convenience format):** Rapid replenishment, small storage footprint, daily cross-dock replenishment.

## 2. Product Family Classification
The product portfolio encompasses 33 official product families:
- **Core Fresh & Perishables:** GROCERY I, BEVERAGES, PRODUCE, DAIRY, MEATS, POULTRY, EGGS, BREAD/BAKERY, DELI, SEAFOOD.
- **Household & Personal Care:** CLEANING, BEAUTY, PERSONAL CARE, BABY CARE.
- **Home, Electronics & Apparel:** HOME CARE, HOME AND KITCHEN I, HOME AND KITCHEN II, HARDWARE, LAWN AND GARDEN, PLAYERS AND ELECTRONICS, LADIESWEAR, MENSWEAR, CELEBRATION.
- **Specialty & Seasonal:** LIQUOR, WINE, BEER, FROZEN FOODS, PREPARED FOODS, SCHOOL AND OFFICE SUPPLIES, BOOKS, MAGAZINES, PET SUPPLIES, AUTOMOTIVE.

## 3. Store Receiving and Put-Away Windows
- Morning Receiving Window: **04:00 - 07:30 local time** (prior to store customer opening at 08:00).
- Evening Inbound Window: **20:30 - 23:00 local time** (following store closing).
- All physical receiving inspections must log temperature compliance for refrigerated perishables and discrepancy counts within 60 minutes of truck arrival.

## 4. Inter-Branch Emergency Stock Transfers
When a store faces a high stockout probability ($> 40\%$) for a critical Class A item and central replenishment will take $> 48$ hours, an emergency store-to-store transfer is authorized under the following conditions:
1. Donating store is within a 25-kilometer transit radius.
2. Donating store retains $\ge 21$ days of forward cover after transfer.
3. Transfer quantity equals minimum 3 days and maximum 7 days of recipient store average daily demand.
4. Logistics cost of transfer does not exceed 10% of gross margin of transferred inventory.

## 5. Holiday and Event Demand Management
- Ecuador Mother's Day, Holy Week, Independence Holidays, and Christmas/New Year represent peak demand inflection points.
- **Christmas Day (December 25):** The entire store network is closed. POS systems and distribution centers are non-operational. Forecasting models must zero-out retail demand on December 25 while accommodating elevated pre-holiday surge across December 20–24.
