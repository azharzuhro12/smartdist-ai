## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 3.2 delivers a production-grade, reproducible demand forecasting system for SmartDist AI. Built upon the Phase 3.1 historical data foundation (648,648 daily sales records covering 54 stores and 33 product families in 2013), this phase developed, tuned, compared, and deployed three model architectures:
1. **Seasonal Naive Baseline** ($T-7$ weekly seasonality lookback).
2. **LightGBM Regressor** (Gradient boosted decision trees with native categorical splits).
3. **XGBoost Regressor** (Histogram-based gradient boosted trees with early stopping).

### Key Accomplishments:
- **Zero Future-Leakage Feature Policy**: Identified and strictly eliminated contemporaneous unobserved store foot-traffic (`transactions`) from the feature set.
- **Model Lift**: On the validation partition, **LightGBM achieved 13.09% WAPE**, outperforming the Seasonal Naive baseline (17.67% WAPE) by **4.58 percentage points** (a **25.9% relative error reduction**), and reducing RMSE from 310.00 to 219.56.
- **Champion Selection**: LightGBM was selected as champion over XGBoost (13.26% WAPE) due to superior aggregate volume accuracy, native categorical handling, and 2x faster training convergence.
- **Unbiased Test Verification**: On the untouched December 2013 test partition (holiday peak demand shock), LightGBM achieved **21.55% WAPE**, substantially beating Seasonal Naive (**27.48% WAPE**) and reducing RMSE from 410.90 to 318.42.
- **Multi-Step Recursive Engine**: Delivered `ForecastingPredictor` supporting 7-day (13.87% WAPE), 14-day (16.72% WAPE), and 30-day (26.12% WAPE) horizons without reading future actuals.
- **Production Infrastructure**: Implemented `BaseForecaster`, `ModelRegistry`, `ModelEvaluator`, serialized artifacts in `ml/artifacts/`, and verified **55 passed tests** across the entire project test suite.

## 2. Feature Availability & Leakage Audit

A comprehensive feature availability audit was conducted to prevent lookahead bias:

| Feature Category | Features | Forecast Time Status | Policy Decision |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Category A: Known in Advance** | Calendar attributes (`day_of_week`, `month`, `is_weekend`), store metadata (`store_type`, `cluster`), catalog metadata (`family`), statutory holiday calendar (`is_holiday`, `holiday_type`). | 100% available at any future horizon $T_0 + h$. | **Approved** for training and inference. |
| **Category B: Historically Observed** | Autoregressive lags (`lag_1`, `lag_7`, `lag_14`, `lag_28`), rolling statistics (`rolling_mean_W`, `rolling_std_W`). | Known at $T_0$. For $T_0 + h$, future values are unobserved. | **Approved** with strict recursive updating using generated predictions $\hat{y}$. |
| **Category C: Operationally Risky** | Customer store foot-traffic (`transactions`), spot promotions, market crude oil prices (`oil_price`). | Same-day foot-traffic is completely unknown at forecast time. | **`transactions` excluded** from feature matrix. `oil_price` forward-filled from $T_0$. |

## 3. Model Implementations

### 3.1 Seasonal Naive Baseline
- Implemented in `ml/models/seasonal_naive.py`.
- Formulates prediction $\hat{y}(s, p, T) = y(s, p, T - 7)$ per `(store_nbr, product_id)`.
- Includes fallback to latest observed demand or 0.0 for unobserved series.
- Enforces non-negative prediction clipping $\max(0, \hat{y})$.

### 3.2 LightGBM Forecaster
- Implemented in `ml/models/lightgbm_model.py`.
- Employs native categorical splits for `store_type`, `holiday_type`, `store_cluster`, and `product_id`.
- Hyperparameters: `n_estimators=400`, `learning_rate=0.05`, `num_leaves=63`, `subsample=0.8`, `colsample_bytree=0.8`, `random_state=42`.
- Utilizes early stopping (`stopping_rounds=30`) against the validation partition.

### 3.3 XGBoost Forecaster
- Implemented in `ml/models/xgboost_model.py`.
- Employs `tree_method="hist"` with `enable_categorical=True`.
- Hyperparameters: `n_estimators=400`, `learning_rate=0.05`, `max_depth=6`, `subsample=0.8`, `colsample_bytree=0.8`, `random_state=42`.
- Utilizes early stopping (`stopping_rounds=30`) against the validation partition.

## 9. Known Limitations

1. **Single Historical Year (2013)**: The current model was trained on 10 months of 2013 data. Multi-year annual seasonality (e.g. Easter shifts, year-over-year macro growth) cannot be captured until subsequent years (2014–2017) are ingested in future data engineering cycles.
2. **Recursive Error Compounding at Horizon 30**: As expected in recursive multi-step forecasting, WAPE degrades from 13.87% (7-day) to 26.12% (30-day). Safety stock calculation modules must account for this horizon-dependent variance.
3. **Macroeconomic Oil Shocks**: The production feature policy forward-fills oil price from $T_0$. Rapid crude oil shocks will introduce exogenous forecast variance.
